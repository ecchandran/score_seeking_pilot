Stage 2 (stage2_dose_v2), control condition. Share of grader readers who violated the multiline instruction, with Wilson 95% intervals. The five OpenAI models (gpt-oss-120b is OpenAI's open-weight model) are ordered by capability (human-supplied order, equally spaced). DeepSeek V4 Pro is shown separately, unranked. Exploratory, not pre-registered.

V = 1.0 All doses V > 0 pooled

Rank correlation with capability, five OpenAI models: at V = 1, Spearman ρ = −0.67 (p = 0.22); pooled over V > 0, ρ = −0.70 (p = 0.19). Without gpt-oss (o3, GPT-5.5, Sol, Astra), at V = 1: ρ = −0.95 (p = 0.05). The decline is carried by the two newest models: older ones violate at almost any positive score.

Capability is confounded with release date, provider and safety training, and there are only six models, so this is a description, not a capability effect.
